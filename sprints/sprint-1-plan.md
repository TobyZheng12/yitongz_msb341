# Sprint 1 Plan

**Goal:** Validate whether to build the medical student test prep app and decide whether to pursue it, and find the right teammate(s) for the team.

**Why this:** I am working with a teammate and still trying to find the right teammate, and I want to find the best project/app with strong market fit and competitive analysis to work on for the rest of the semester.

**Done looks like:** We have a good team, and we know whether we are continuing with the medical student test prep app or pivoting to a different idea, based on how many people we interviewed and what the results were.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3

**Actual difficulty:** 4

**Why it differed:** Finding people willing to talk to us was harder than expected, and
running the interviews in English, not our first language, made it more difficult than

pivot from an MCAT-specific test prep app to a broader study-strategy app, because we found

teammate, Shulin, but still need an American teammate, since we're both international
students and can't legally run the business ourselves. Next sprint: keep interviewing (a
survey is planned), keep recruiting a teammate, and Shulin keeps improving the prototype based
on what we learn.
