# Usability test: 2026-10-26

**Who:** A BYU student, returned missionary who speaks Chinese and has been to China.
**Task I gave them:** On their own phone, use the PengPal prototype to find a local friend they
like.
**Prototype:** https://shulinzzz.github.io/MSB-341_Shulin_Repo/product/pengpal/

## What they did

Used the prototype without interruption from us and found a local friend they liked. We asked
our prepared questions about traveling in China.

## Where they got stuck

Did not get stuck finding or booking a local friend. Like the two participants on Oct 21, was
unsure how to contact the local friend after booking to coordinate before the trip.

## What I am changing because of this

Nothing yet. This is the third participant to raise the same post-booking question.
