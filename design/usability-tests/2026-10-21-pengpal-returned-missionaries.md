# Usability test: 2026-10-21

**Who:** Two BYU students, both returned missionaries who speak Chinese. Participant A has been
to China 2 to 4 times. Participant B has been to China once, on a trip with me. Session ran about
one hour, both together.
**Task I gave them:** On their own phone, use the PengPal prototype to find a local friend they
like.
**Prototype:** https://shulinzzz.github.io/MSB-341_Shulin_Repo/product/pengpal/

## What they did

Both used the prototype without interruption from us and found a local friend they liked. Before
and after the task we asked our prepared questions about traveling in China.

Participant A said (paraphrased, not word for word) that in almost every city he visited in
China, he could find a friend who was from there, because of all the connections he made on his
mission, and that this helped him a lot and made his visits much easier.

## What they actually do today

They find local help through people they already know: friends from their mission who live in,
or are from, the city they are visiting.

## Where they got stuck

Neither got stuck finding or booking a local friend. Both were unsure what happens after booking:
what the best way is to contact the local friend before the trip to coordinate.

## What I am changing because of this

Nothing yet. The gap after booking is noted for the next prototype iteration.
