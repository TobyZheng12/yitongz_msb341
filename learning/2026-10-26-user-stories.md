## What we covered

- Predict: an agent told only "Add a search bar" builds the wrong search. The missing pieces were the user (who) and the benefit (why), not placement.
- The format: As a [user] (who), I want [goal] (what), so that [benefit] (why). A feature names a thing; a story names an outcome.
- New case: "As a user, I want a dark mode button." Correctly spotted the missing "so that." Different "so that" reasons lead to different builds, and the why can show the button is not needed at all. "As a user" is also too vague.
- Started acceptance criteria: conditions that must be true for a story to be done, and the list you check the agent's work against. Stopped before answering the predict question about leaving out "Old password no longer works."
