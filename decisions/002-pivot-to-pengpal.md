# Decision 002: Drop the student study app and build PengPal

**Date:** 2026-10-08 (recorded 2026-10-12)
**Status:** Active
**Supersedes:** [001](001-pivot-from-mcat-focus.md)

## Context

Sprint 2 was meant to interview more pre-med students, improve the study-prep prototype, and
decide whether to pivot. Two things settled the decision:

1. Interviews with pre-med students and a full interview with the BYU pre-professional advisor
   did not support the test prep direction.
2. OpenAI launched Muse and Dots during the sprint. Both can do what our study/test prep
   product was planning to do, so a two-person student team has no defensible position there.

## Options considered

1. **Keep building the study-strategy / MCAT prep app**: pros — we had a prototype and three
   interviews behind it; cons — Muse and Dots can replace it outright, and neither the students
   nor the pre-professional advisor gave us a reason to think otherwise.
2. **Build PengPal**: pros — connects foreign travelers in China with local Chinese people who
   speak their language; a rising market, and Chinese locals genuinely want to meet foreigners;
   it is also something we know firsthand as Chinese students who have hosted foreign friends;
   cons — a two-sided marketplace is harder to start than a study tool, and we have no product
   built yet.
3. **Join a prospective teammate's project** (carried over from Decision 001): pros — would
   bring a third teammate with it; cons — no candidate project emerged this sprint.

## Decision

Drop the student study app and build PengPal. The deciding reason: a general-purpose AI
assistant can replace a study tool, but it cannot be a local person in Shanghai who meets you at
the restaurant.

We committed to it by entering PengPal in the Big Idea Competition (deadline October 8, 2026)
with a pitch deck and video, and wrote a business brief for our advisor.

## What would change our mind

If the Big Idea Competition feedback or the first traveler and local interviews show the
two-sided marketplace cannot be started at our scale, or if liability and trust problems around
matching strangers in a foreign country turn out to be worse than we think.
