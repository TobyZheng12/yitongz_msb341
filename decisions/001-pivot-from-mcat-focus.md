# Decision 001: Broaden from MCAT-specific test prep to a general study-strategy app

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Status:** Active

## Context

Three interviews with pre-med / college students validated interest in a study-help product
and confirmed the audience is price-sensitive, but also surfaced that neither founder has the
domain depth in medical school admissions or the MCAT needed to build credible,
subject-specific content.

## Options considered

1. **Stay MCAT/medical-school specific**: pros — narrower, clearer audience; cons — neither
   founder has the subject-matter depth to build trustworthy content, high risk of a shallow
   or low-credibility product.
2. **Broaden to a general study-strategy app**: pros — plays to what the founders actually
   know and are interested in, still serves the same price-sensitive student audience; cons —
   less differentiated, audience definition gets less specific.
3. **Drop this idea and join a prospective teammate's project**: pros — could pair with a new
   (American) teammate's existing idea; cons — loses momentum on interest already validated.

## Decision

Broaden the product to a general study-strategy app rather than staying MCAT-specific.
Continue recruiting a teammate — ideally American, since both current members are
international students and are not permitted to run a business themselves — and stay open to
also exploring a prospective teammate's own project idea in parallel with this one.

## What would change our mind

If the teammate search surfaces a stronger, already-validated idea from a prospective
teammate, or if further interviews show the study-strategy angle isn't differentiated enough
to be viable.
